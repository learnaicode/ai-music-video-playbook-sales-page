I've never made a music video. Is this for me?

Yes. You start with a 10 second test and a 30 second film. You need a song you have the rights to, any editor, and one image and one video generator.

No. The NAMA films used Suno, Higgsfield and Seedance, but any equivalent tools work. The book tells you what to check before you pay for a plan.
