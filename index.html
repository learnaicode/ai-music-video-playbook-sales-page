See the difference

The prompt that saves the shot

Adjectives don't direct a model. A director's prompt asks for one action and one end state, so the shot can be cut. This one is from Day 3 of the book.

✕ What most people type"A cinematic, epic, emotional shot of a warrior holding fire on a mountain, 8K, masterpiece, dramatic lighting."

Nothing to cut on. Nothing to check. You'll pay for it twice.
✓ The director's prompt · Day 3SHOT ID F03 · PURPOSE the decision to give · INPUT approved character reference C01, location reference L01 · FRAME medium close-up, eye level, static camera · ACTION the figure opens his right hand slowly; a small golden flame remains above the palm · LIGHT soft warm source from screen-left · CONTINUITY dark shoulder-length hair; cream drape; bronze shoulder detail · END STATE open palm held still; eyes on the flame · AVOID new props, cuts, extra people, camera orbit · ACCEPT IF hand, face and flame remain readable
1One action per shot. Need two? Make two shots.
2Describe what the camera sees, not what the viewer should feel.
3Repeat the continuity line word for word.
4Write the end state, or you'll pay for the shot twice.
5Change one thing per retry, and write it down.

Questions

Frequently asked

I have never made a music video. Is this for me?

Yes. You start with a 10 second test and a 30 second first film, not a full song. You need a song you have the right to use, any editor that can cut to music, and one image and one video generator. The book shows the smallest plans to start with.

Does it only work with Suno and Higgsfield?

No. The NAMA films used Suno, Higgsfield and Seedance, and the book shows that chain. The method doesn't depend on a brand: any equivalent tool works, and the book tells you what to check before you pay for a plan.

Can I see the films before I buy?

Yes. All 14 are free on YouTube at youtube.com/@namaguru. Start with Prometheus, the film the book's studio story follows.

Can I read some of it first?

Yes. The Playbook's product page shows the opening and all of Day 1 free (19 pages). The bundle page shows the opening of both books.

Is this a printed book? How do I get it?

It's a digital download. Right after payment you get the files on screen and by email. The bundle arrives as one ZIP: tap it and choose Extract (Files app on iPhone, Files by Google on Android). Nothing is shipped.

Can I really type into the Workbook?

Yes. The DOCX opens in Microsoft Word, Apple Pages or Google Docs. Save a fresh copy for each film. Prefer paper? Print the PDF version.

Will it get me views or income?

No book can promise that, and this one doesn't. It teaches you to finish and release a film that holds together, and to read your analytics honestly so the next one is better.

Will the tools change and make this outdated?

Tools change monthly. The method (map the song, decide the story, control continuity, cut on change, finish properly) doesn't. The book is written so you translate each step into whatever your tool offers today.

Can I use what I learn in client work?

Yes. The licence lets you use the prompts, sheets and templates in your own projects, including paid client work. Please don't share or resell the files.
